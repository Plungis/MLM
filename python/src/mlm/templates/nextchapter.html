✨ How to Get Your Free Google Gemini API Key

NextChapter is powered exclusively by Google Gemini Flash. Regular expressions fail to understand what book titles actually mean (subtitles, unnumbered novellas, spin-offs, and future sequels). Gemini uses comprehensive literary intelligence to analyze your series, map real volume numbers to official titles, and find missing books.

  1. Open Google AI Studio API Keys (aistudio.google.com/app/apikey) ↗ in a new tab.
  2. Sign in with your Google account.
  3. Click the blue "Create API Key" button (100% free with generous daily quotas).
  4. Copy your key (starts with AIzaSy...) and paste it into the Google Gemini API Key field below.
  5. Click "Save NextChapter Settings", then navigate to Series Hub and click "Scan Series Now"!
🔒 Your API key is saved locally in your private config.toml and is only used to query the Gemini API for your book series.
